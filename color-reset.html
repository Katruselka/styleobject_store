The method

Same colors.
New outfit.

Change the placement before you change the wardrobe.

01 — Choose your pair. Find two pieces in colors that work together.

02 — Switch the balance. Try the lighter color on top, then below.

03 — Add one support. Use a third color in one layer or two accessories.

Use the closest shades you already own. You do not need an exact color match.

Formula 01

Flip the colors.

BUTTER
BURGUNDY

What: Butter + burgundy.

How: Switch the colors between top and bottom.

Why: A new balance changes the whole outfit.

Build the look: Tailored / navy layer or relaxed / taupe details. Keep the third color in one layer or two small accessories.

Formula 02

Move the color.

CHARCOAL
CORAL

What: Charcoal + coral.

How: Move coral from the jacket to the trousers.

Why: The strongest color becomes a new focal point.

Build the look: Color on top or color below. Let coral be the only bright color.

Formula 03

Reverse the balance.

CHOCOLATE
SKY BLUE

What: Chocolate + sky blue.

How: Try blue above the waist, then below it.

Why: The same pair works in different proportions.

Build the look: Blue shirt / cream shoes or blue skirt / taupe details. Match the color family, not the exact shade.

Your next step

From color to complete outfits.

Proportion: Does the length of the top work with the skirt or trousers?

Finishing: Do the shoes, bag and belt repeat a color already in the outfit?

Real life: Can you walk, sit and move comfortably in the complete look?

Color is the starting point. Fit, proportions and finishing details complete the outfit.
